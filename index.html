PROJECTS

Research & Innovation

01
Bachelor Final Year Project

Production of Bioethanol from Waste Office Paper

2023 – 2024

This project focused on converting waste office paper into bioethanol as a sustainable alternative to conventional fuels. As the project leader, I coordinated the team through the preparation and pretreatment of waste paper, enzymatic hydrolysis to release fermentable sugars, and fermentation using yeast to produce ethanol. The project involved process design, experimental optimization, data analysis, and evaluation of factors affecting ethanol yield. It demonstrated the potential of transforming an abundant paper waste stream into a valuable renewable fuel while supporting waste reduction and circular-economy principles.

As the project leader, I coordinated a multidisciplinary team and contributed to the conception, design and implementation of a process that transforms discarded paper into a valuable source of bioethanol.
